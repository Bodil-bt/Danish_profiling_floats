Danish profiling floats — status

Live status of the profiling floats operated and monitored by DTU Aqua, in Greenland waters and in Danish/Baltic waters.

About

DTU (Aqua) is currently operating a fleet of profiling oceanographic floats. These contribute to global climate monitoring (part of the global Argo program), water quality monitoring and experimental/research data collection. The floats are designed to drift with ocean currents, parking at depth between cycles and rising to the surface every few days to transmit a profile of the water column. Here we adapt the performance by parking on the seafloor so they perform as virtual moorings. Sensors on the floats vary and measure a range of parameters including temperature, salinity, oxygen, phytoplankton fluorescence, particle scattering, organic matter fluorescence, and spectral light intensity. This page summarises where our floats are, how many profiles they have returned, and when each is next expected at the surface.

The equipment has been financed by the national infrastructure project GIOS (Greenland Integrated Observatory), Strategic Funds from DTU (RSO), Integrated Marine Monitoring project by Agency for Green Transition and Aquatic Environment, and Danish Defence and Logistics.
